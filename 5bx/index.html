          Voice cues

          Speaks the exercise, its target and the jump sets, so you don’t have to look at the screen. Uses your device’s built-in voice — no network needed.

          This browser has no speech support, so voice cues will stay silent.

          Time to get up off the floor and set up. It sits outside the 11 minutes — the app tracks exercise time and total time separately. With voice cues on, the exercise never starts while the announcement is still speaking.

          Step metronome
